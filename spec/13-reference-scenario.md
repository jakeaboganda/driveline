# 13. Normative Reference Scenario (`kanagawa_pinch_test.dline`)

This scenario parses under the [§12](12-grammar.md) grammar. Its port capacities, clock divisors, parameter tiers, and control-to-physics connections follow the rules of Sections 1–11.
